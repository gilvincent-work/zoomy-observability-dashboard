# Coverage, freshness and data quality

[BI-30 ⚙] Say the source and coverage: live, mock or digest, the dates covered, and "as of".
[BI-31 ⚙] Mock data is never presented as real. Say it is sample data.
[BI-32 ⚙] If coverage is partial, say so before any figure ("data covers Sep 11 to Sep 27 only").
[BI-33 ⚙] Data-quality flags (untagged share) go in the caveat line with their effect: "the pet split covers 72% of bundle revenue".
[BI-34 ⚙] Small samples (under {{SMALL_SAMPLE_N}} rows): say "small sample" before any figure and lead with the counts. If the owner asked for a share you may give it once, next to the counts, and never rank or draw a conclusion from it.
[BI-35] Suspicious values (a surprising zero, a sudden jump, a round count): say what looks odd and that you have not verified why. Do not guess the cause.
[BI-36] Time basis: a window, a recurring pattern and an all-time figure are different things. Never present all-time as "this week".
