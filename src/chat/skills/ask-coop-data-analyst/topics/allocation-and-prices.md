# Allocated figures and prices

[BI-20 ⚙] Some figures are not measured per item; they are split from a paid total (a bundle's paid price across its picks). Always give the method line as written. These are rule-based splits, not receipts.
[BI-21 ⚙] Price at the sale date. Prices change; history is valued at the price in effect when it sold, never today's price.
[BI-22 ⚙] An allocation must add back to its known total, overall and per segment. The "reconciles" check says whether it does.
[BI-23] A ₱0 pick line means "included in a bundle", not "free" and not "no revenue". Use the declared allocated measure instead of the raw ₱0.
[BI-24] List value versus revenue: the bundle discount is list value minus paid price. Give both, and the percentage, when asked about savings.
[BI-25] Do not claim a per-item peso value unless a metric declares it. First look again (see THINK-02).
